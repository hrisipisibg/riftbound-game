# Gameplay

## Characters and progression

Choose a faction, race and one of the current classes: Warrior, Paladin, Mage or Rogue. The character set includes Human, Dwarf, Elf, Orc, Undead and Goblin. Progress through quests, combat, exploration and equipment upgrades. Subclasses and additional classes remain future work.

Abe introduces the road-raider, stolen-supply and Ashwood Captain quest chain. Quartermaster Rowan and Provisioner Mira provide supplies. Cartographer Elara at Greenhollow offers frontier surveys followed by the River Reaver bounty. Captain Selvek remains a hostile enemy; defeating him and claiming linked rewards follow the existing quest/chest rules.

## Travel

Running is the normal movement mode; C toggles slow walking. Z summons/dismisses the selected unlocked mount and V cycles mounts. Movement, damage and incompatible actions can interrupt a summon. Mount availability and exploration requirements still apply.

Travel between Emberwatch, Highspire, Westbank and the Reaver camp, or north to Greenhollow. Test bridge decks, steep approaches and camera clearance when reporting movement defects; a location on the map is not proof it is traversable.

## Groups

Characters start alone. G opens optional companions; O opens group/session controls. Online sessions support up to four human players through a private invite code. Peer frames show supported health/resource/status information; ready checks and pings help coordinate. Social leadership does not transfer transport hosting.

Online progress is session-only. Leaving a session does not overwrite offline progress, and host departure ends the session. Use the same complete build on every participant's PC.

## Reporting problems

Record build/source, offline/host/client role, race/class, location, action and expected/actual result. Foot/seat/facing problems benefit from a short video. Keep invite codes and authentication information out of shared reports. A rendered pose or scripted test is not a substitute for normal-input playtesting.
